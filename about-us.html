About Us

About

This website is for gamers and individuals that wish to try games. Featuring Game News to keep you updated on what's happening in the Philippines Gaming Community, Featured Filipino-made Games that we personally reviewed to showcase you what it is about and why it is fun and more. The website provides everything you need to understand, catch up on trends and find new games that might intrigue your interest.

Our Mission and Purpose is to inform everyone, old gamers or new gamers the information they need to start their gaming journey and keep up with the trend while also showing support and love to our country by reviewing and showcasing interesting Filipino-made games.
