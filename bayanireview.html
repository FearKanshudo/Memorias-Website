Bayani

Score: 9.0

Platform: PC
Genre: Action, Indie
Release Date: June 12, 2019
Reviewed by: Roland Mar Belarmente

Pros

  • Unique Cultural Concept
  • Accessible Combat
  • Fun Mechanics

Cons

  • Unbalanced Damage Scaling
  • Lack Character Roster
  • Barebones Training Mode
  • Unpolished Audio Design

Full Review

I went to this game looking for gems, and what I found certainly filled me with joy. Not only did I learn some more about the history of our heroes, I also found more cause and reasons on why Philippines is a tough country.

Though, I can certainly say the game is definitely not easy. It's also like your average Tekken game. It's definitely not easy to get a hand in.

Overall i rate it a 7.3, the game is good and content appropriate, really cheap too but some features are a bit hard to do and a chore such as the payment and change which is accurate in real-life but videogame wise, it's lacking some QoL (Quality of Life) features.
