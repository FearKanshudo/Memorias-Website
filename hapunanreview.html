Hapunan

Score: 8.8

Platform: PC
Genre: Horror, Indie, Psychological Horror
Release Date: August 25, 2026
Reviewed by: Meshari Barraquias

Pros

  • Filipino Setting and Atmosphere
  • Creepy Sound Design
  • Simple but Engaging Gameplay
  • Strong Horror Experience

Cons

  • Short Playtime
  • Limited Gameplay Variety

Full Review

I went into Hapunan expecting a simple horror game, but the Filipino setting made the experience feel much more familiar and unsettling. Instead of relying only on constant jump scares, the game creates tension through its dark environment, strange sounds, and the feeling that something could appear at any moment.

What I enjoyed most was the atmosphere. The setting feels like a place that could actually exist in the Philippines, which makes the horror easier to connect with. Exploring the dark rooms while searching for clues kept me interested, especially when I wasn't sure what would happen next.

The gameplay is simple, but it works well for a short horror experience. My main complaint is that the game can feel too short, and there isn't a huge variety of things to do. Still, Hapunan shows that a simple Filipino setting can create an effective horror experience. Overall, it's a creepy and enjoyable game that is worth trying, especially for players who enjoy Filipino horror.
