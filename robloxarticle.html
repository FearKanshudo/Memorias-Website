REMULLA CLAIMS THE SHOOTING AT ATENEO DE ZAMBOANGA WAS CAUSED BY ROBLOX

By Matthew Jeremie Ramos
09/11/2026

A Grade 8, 14 yrs old male student opened fire on the campus while livestreaming the act on another website apart from his facebook account. Interior and Local Government Secretary Jonvic Remulla stated that the online gaming platform Roblox may have influenced the student involved in the fatal shooting at Ateneo de Zamboanga University.

Remulla said “The child was a very exceptional student. Number one in his class, never had behavioral problems. We are going to go through all his gadgets to ascertain the facts,”.

“But, it is with great degree of confidence that he was influenced by the game of Roblox again because of the manner in which he did it. Roblox in itself is not a dangerous game. It is the chat group that is inside it. Doon nagkakaroon ng radicalization (That's where radicalization comes in),” he added.

He stated that witness have claimed to seen the shooter playing Roblox before the incident occured, Authorities are now investigating the website on which the shooter supposedly livestreamed the incident.

“It gives us a scenario as to his intent to show the world what he was doing. The version he was doing was akin to a video game, a first-person shooter. A camera was attached to his chest and he was livestreaming the incident, In my view, he was recreating the scenario he saw on video games in real life. So, that's the only realistic explanation we saw,” he added.

Developers of Roblox was in a joint statement with the Philippines last April due to a completely different matter, Lack of security and explicit content in which Roblox responded by adding new account types to age-check children and provide age-appropriate content.
