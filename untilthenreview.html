Until Then

Score: 9.7

Platform: PC, Consoles, Nintendo Switch
Genre: Casual, Indie
Release Date: June 25, 2024
Reviewed by: Jehoakim Mena

Pros

  • Amazing Writing.
  • Stylized Graphics.
  • Cultural Experience.

Cons

  • Slowburn Pacing.
  • Minimal Choice Effects.

Full Review

I went into this game blind attracted by the pixel art style and then i got blown away with the story, world and specifically the characters and how they interacted it felt really immersive. The characters were really relatable and what they did and said was what i would have done and said too they really felt like actual people i was able to predict roughly what they would say sometimes too.

The story and plot was an entire emotional rollercoaster i have never gone through all emotions in a game like this before i highly recommend playing it and being immersed getting into the mind of the characters so you can understand them and acting like you are actually mark the main character for the best experience.
